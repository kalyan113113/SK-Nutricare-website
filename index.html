About SK Nutricare™

Nutrition. Wellness. Transformation.

SK Nutricare™ is a comprehensive nutrition coaching platform built to make expert, personalized health guidance accessible to everyone — right from their phone.

🎯
Our Vision

To be India's most trusted nutrition coaching platform — where every individual has access to personalized, science-backed dietary guidance that transforms their health and quality of life.

🌿
Our Mission

To deliver holistic, personalized nutrition coaching through technology — combining clinical expertise, real-time coaching, and a powerful app to help clients achieve lasting health transformations.

Who We Are

SK Nutricare™ was founded with a simple but powerful belief — that good nutrition should be personal, accessible, and effective. We are a team of qualified nutrition and wellness professionals dedicated to your health journey.

Led by Dt. Swetha Gangadhari, our platform brings together clinical nutrition, fitness, and wellness coaching under one roof — delivered through a cutting-edge mobile app.
