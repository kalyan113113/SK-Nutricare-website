2. Information We Collect

We collect the following information, most of it directly from you when you sign up and use the app:

Account details: name, email address, phone number (required when you sign up), gender and date of birth. You sign in with a one-time code (OTP) sent to your email, or with Google or Apple (Apple sign-in is available on iPhone).

Body and health profile: height, weight, goal weight, activity level, health goal, medical conditions, allergies, pregnancy status, and lifestyle details such as smoking, alcohol, stress level, work type, and sleep and wake times.

Food and activity: diet preferences and restrictions, meals you log (food items, quantities and nutritional data), water intake, steps and activity, and sleep.

Health trackers: HbA1c readings; thyroid values (TSH, T3, T4), symptoms and your medication-reminder setting; period / menstrual cycle dates and symptoms; and mood, energy, stress and cravings. Each entry can include a date and notes.

Progress photos and body measurements.

Messages: chat messages with your coach and any images or PDF files you or your coach send in the chat.

Coaching records: appointments (dates, times and consultation notes), notes, goals, follow-ups, reminders and diet plans prepared for you by your coach, and any rating or feedback you give about your coach.

Payments: the plan you buy, amount, dates, payment method and transaction references. Online payments are handled by Razorpay; we do NOT see or store your card number, UPI PIN or banking passwords. When a payment link is created for you, your name, email and phone number are shared with Razorpay for that payment. All financial transactions are routed through our parent entity, Rudraksh Enterprises. Please note that transaction charges on your bank statements, UPI apps, or credit card bills will appear under the name "Rudraksh Enterprises".

Device and sign-in activity: your push notification token, and a record of each sign-in (time, IP address, device or browser details and whether it succeeded). This helps us keep accounts secure.

Call records: when our team places a call to you through the app, we keep a record of the call (time, who called, the number or name called, how long it lasted, the outcome) and any notes our staff write about it. We store no call audio. TeleCMI, our telephony provider, may keep its own call records under its own privacy policy.

App error and performance reports (see "Third-party services" below), which can include basic technical details about your device and what the app was doing when an error happened.

Website visits: our website home page uses the Meta (Facebook) Pixel to measure advertising, and both website pages load fonts from Google Fonts. These services may receive your IP address and browser details when you visit.

3. Health Readings Entered by Your Coach

Your coach, and the CEO of SK Nutricare™, can enter health tracker readings on your behalf (for example an HbA1c, thyroid, period or mood entry). When they do, you receive a push notification.

Your information is shared only with:

6. Third-Party Services

We use the following services. Each has its own privacy policy, which we encourage you to review.

Razorpay: online payments; receives your name, email and phone number to create a payment link

Brevo: sends our emails (sign-in codes, payment links and similar); a backup email service may be used if Brevo is unavailable

Cloudinary: stores the images and PDF files sent in chat

MongoDB Atlas: cloud database where your account and app data are stored

Render: hosts our servers

Expo: builds and updates the app and delivers push notifications

Firebase (Google): Remote Config on Android, which delivers app settings to the app

TeleCMI: connects calls placed by our team

Google and Apple: only if you choose to sign in with them

Open Food Facts: the food names you search for are sent to it to find nutrition information

Meta (Facebook) Pixel and Google Fonts: on our website, as described above

7. Data Storage & Security

Your data is stored in a cloud database run by our service providers (see above)

Communication between the app and our servers is encrypted using HTTPS

Any password we store is hashed (not stored in readable form)

Sign-in tokens expire automatically, and signing in on a new device signs you out of the old one

Access to client data is limited by role

No system is completely secure, but we work to protect your information.

8. Deleting Your Account & Data Retention

We keep your data for as long as your account is active.

To delete your account in the app: go to Profile and choose "Delete my account". This erases your personal data immediately, including your account, meals, water and activity logs, health trackers, progress photos and measurements, chat messages and uploaded files, notifications, sign-in history, plans and diet plans, goals, reminders and coach notes about you.

To request deletion by email: write to swetha.g@sknutricare.in with the subject "Account Deletion Request" and the email address you registered with. We process email requests as soon as we have received them and confirmed it is really you.

What we keep after deletion:

Payment, invoice and transaction records are retained as required by law. After you delete your account, your name, email and any notes are removed from them.

Business records of calls and sales enquiries (such as that a call happened and its outcome) are kept without your name, phone number or notes.

Database backups are kept for up to 14 days, so deleted data may remain in a backup until it is overwritten, within that period.

Records already held by our payment, calling and other service providers follow their own policies.
